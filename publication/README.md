### Data

Primary option dataset: `rissin/nse-options-intraday`, pinned revision `8f7739cab3f38abdcbc6332a6d0a83e1341326e3`.

The publication does not redistribute the large raw option dataset. It records the immutable source revision and provenance.

### Scientific interpretation

The manuscript reports positive historical sample means for both indices under the locked primary assumptions, but the primary confidence intervals include zero, NIFTY is calibration-sensitive, tail losses are substantial, and exact historical broker/exchange margin is not reconstructed. The study therefore does not claim a stable or production-ready trading edge.

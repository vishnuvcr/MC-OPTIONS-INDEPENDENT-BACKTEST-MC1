## Corporate actions
The traded instruments are cash-settled index options. Constituent-level corporate actions are therefore not directly modeled as option-contract adjustments; their effect on the index level is embedded in the observed index history. Index reconstitutions and dividend effects remain a potential source of regime variation rather than a separate trade-level adjustment.

## News
News is not used as a predictive variable. The Phase 6 market-context layer uses observable market-price/volatility variables to avoid discretionary look-ahead from narrative event labeling.

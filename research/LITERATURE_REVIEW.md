# Literature Review Index

## Indian option-market evidence
- Garg & Vipul (2015), Journal of Futures Markets: Indian option volatility-risk-premium forecasts and the effect of transaction costs.
- Dynamics of variance risk premium: Evidence from India (Research in International Business and Finance): NIFTY option risk-premium evidence and robustness across volatility forecasts/subsamples.
- Pillai (2026), SSRN: NIFTY volatility-risk-premium strategies evaluated with explicit transaction costs.
- John (2026), preprint: NIFTY option volatility-risk-premium backtest with explicit post-2024 regulatory-regime separation.

## Transaction-cost / simulation foundations
- Boyle (1992), Journal of Finance: discrete-time option replication with proportional transaction costs.
- Toft (2009), Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis: option replication cost/risk tradeoffs under transaction costs.
- Chidambaran (2007), Review of Quantitative Finance and Accounting: Monte Carlo density estimation for option arbitrage under transaction costs.

## Implications for this study
The review supports chronology-preserving backtests, explicit execution frictions, regime segmentation, tail-risk reporting, and sensitivity analysis.
